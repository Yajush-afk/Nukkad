# Nukkad

Your neighbourhood is a map you haven’t finished.

Nukkad is a local neighbourhood discovery app. Give it your interests and a little time; it uses an open-weight model to suggest nearby places, calculates a walking route, and creates a card you can take outside on your phone or on paper.

After the walk, record the places you reached and what you noticed. Your personal map and reviewed interests help shape future quests. Visits are self-reported, and map data cannot guarantee access or safety.

Personal notes stay on your laptop. OpenStreetMap supplies the geography, local code plans the walk, and Ollama runs the model. After setup, the core experience is designed to work without an internet connection.

Built for the DEV Hacktoberfest “Touch Grass” challenge.
